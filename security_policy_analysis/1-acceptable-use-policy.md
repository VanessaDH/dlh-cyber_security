## 1. Purpose

TechSecure Solutions Inc. ("TechSecure", "the Company") provides its workforce with information systems — cloud-based development environments, corporate email, VPN, collaboration platforms, devices, and network access — for the purpose of conducting Company business. These systems carry the Company's intellectual property, its source code, and information entrusted to it by its clients. Their misuse, whether deliberate or careless, exposes the Company to data breach, malware infection, legal liability, regulatory penalty, contractual breach with clients, and reputational damage.

This policy exists to:

1. Define clearly what constitutes acceptable and unacceptable use of Company information systems, so that every User knows the boundary before they cross it.
2. Protect Company and client information — particularly source code, product roadmaps, and client data — from unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or loss.
3. Protect Users themselves by setting out, in advance, the rules against which their conduct will be measured and the monitoring to which they are subject.
4. Establish a controlled process for the introduction of software, personal devices, and third-party services into the Company environment.
5. Support TechSecure's obligations under ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II, applicable data protection law (including GDPR where applicable), and client contractual commitments.

**Context for this issue.** This policy is issued in direct response to four recurring patterns identified by management during 2026: routine use of corporate email for personal purposes; installation of unapproved software on Company systems; disclosure of confidential project information on social media; and connection of unregistered personal devices to the corporate network. Sections 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, and 4.5 respectively address these issues.

## 2. Scope

**2.1 Persons covered.** This policy applies to every individual granted access to TechSecure information systems, referred to throughout as "Users":

- Permanent, fixed-term, and part-time employees at all three office locations
- Fully remote and hybrid workers
- Contractors, consultants, and agency staff
- Interns, apprentices, and work-experience placements
- Third-party vendors, partners, and client personnel granted access
- Any individual using a personal device to access Company data under Section 4.5

**2.2 Systems and assets covered.** This policy applies to all information, systems, devices, and services owned, leased, licensed, or administered by TechSecure, and to any personal asset used to access them, including:

- Cloud-based development environments, source code repositories, CI/CD pipelines, and build infrastructure
- Corporate email and calendaring
- VPN, remote access, and Zero Trust network access services
- Collaboration platforms (chat, video conferencing, document sharing, ticketing, wikis)
- Company-issued laptops, desktops, mobile phones, tablets, and peripherals
- Wired and wireless corporate networks at all three offices, and guest networks
- Company-procured SaaS applications and cloud tenancy (IaaS/PaaS)
- All Company and client data in any form, in any location, on any medium
- Personally owned devices registered under the BYOD provisions of Section 4.5
- Company social media accounts and any personal account on which a User identifies their employment with TechSecure

**2.3 Geographic and temporal scope.** This policy applies at all Company premises, at client sites, at home, in transit, and in any public location; during and outside working hours; and in every jurisdiction in which TechSecure operates. It continues to apply to confidentiality obligations after employment or engagement ends.

**2.4 Exclusions.** Personal devices that never access Company data, never connect to a Company network, and are used exclusively for personal purposes are outside the scope of this policy.

## 3. General Principles

3.1 **Company property.** All Company information systems, and all data created, stored, sent, or received on them, are the property of TechSecure Solutions Inc. Users have **no expectation of privacy** in any data stored on, transmitted through, or generated by Company systems, subject to Section 3.4.

3.2 **Business purpose.** Company information systems are provided for business use. Limited, incidental personal use is permitted only under the conditions set out in Sections 4.1 and 4.2.

3.3 **Duty of care.** Every User is personally responsible for the security of the accounts, devices, and data entrusted to them, and for exercising sound judgement where this policy does not address a situation explicitly. Where a User is uncertain whether an action is acceptable, they **must** ask the Information Security Team before proceeding, not after.

3.4 **Monitoring and privacy.** TechSecure monitors, logs, inspects, and retains records of use of its information systems, as detailed in Section 5. Monitoring is conducted lawfully and proportionately for defined security, compliance, and operational purposes; it is not conducted for general surveillance of Users' personal lives. Where local law affords Users additional privacy rights (including under GDPR), the Company applies the stricter standard, and the Employee Privacy Notice (POL-HR-009) governs the handling of the resulting personal data.

3.5 **No circumvention.** Users **must not** attempt to bypass, disable, or defeat any security control, monitoring mechanism, filter, or access restriction, nor assist another person in doing so.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Internet Usage

**4.1.1 Acceptable use.** Company internet access is provided for business purposes: research, documentation, vendor and client interaction, professional development, and approved cloud services.

**4.1.2 Permitted personal use.** Limited personal browsing is permitted during breaks and outside working hours, provided it:

- does not exceed **30 minutes cumulatively per working day**;
- does not interfere with the User's duties or the duties of others;
- does not consume significant bandwidth (streaming video, torrents, large downloads, cryptocurrency operations);
- does not incur cost to the Company;
- does not involve any category prohibited under 4.1.3.

Personal use is a privilege, not an entitlement, and may be restricted or withdrawn by the Company at any time, in general or for an individual.

**4.1.3 Prohibited use.** Users **must not** use Company internet access, from any Company system or network, to access, transmit, download, store, or distribute:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Illegal material | Content depicting child sexual abuse; material inciting terrorism or violence; content whose possession is a criminal offence in the applicable jurisdiction |
| Infringing material | Pirated software, media, or licensed content; circumvention of digital rights management |
| Offensive material | Pornographic, sexually explicit, obscene, or gambling content; material that harasses, defames, or discriminates on the basis of any protected characteristic |
| Malicious material | Malware, exploit kits, hacking tools (except by the Security Team under written authorization), credential-stuffing lists |
| Anonymization and evasion | Personal VPNs, Tor, open proxies, or any service used to bypass Company network monitoring or filtering |
| Unsanctioned services | Personal cloud storage (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud), unapproved file-transfer services, unapproved AI/LLM services when handling Company or client data (see 4.6.6) |
| Commercial misuse | Operating a personal business; unauthorized solicitation, advertising, or fundraising; cryptocurrency mining |

**4.1.4 Web filtering.** The Company operates a web content filter and a DNS security service. Users **must not** attempt to circumvent them. A User who believes a business-necessary site is wrongly blocked **must** request an unblock through the Service Desk rather than seek a workaround.

**4.1.5 Public and untrusted networks.** When working remotely, Users **must** connect through the Company VPN before accessing any Company system. Users **must not** conduct Company business over open public Wi-Fi without the VPN active, and **must not** connect Company devices to networks operated by unknown third parties where a mobile hotspot is available as an alternative.

**4.1.6 Monitoring notice.** All internet traffic traversing Company networks and Company-managed devices is logged, including source user and device, destination domain, timestamp, and volume. TLS inspection is applied to categories of traffic defined in the Network Monitoring Standard, with health, financial, and legal categories excluded from inspection. Logs are retained for **12 months**.

### 4.2 Email Usage

**4.2.1 Acceptable use.** The corporate email system is provided for business communication. All email sent from a `@techsecure.com` address is an official Company communication and may be disclosed in litigation, regulatory investigation, or client audit. Users **must** write accordingly.

**4.2.2 Personal use — the standing issue.** Incidental personal email is tolerated within the limits below, but the Company has identified sustained misuse of corporate email for personal purposes as a material risk. Effective on the date of this policy:

- Users **must not** use their corporate email address to register for, or log in to, any personal account, service, subscription, retailer, social platform, financial service, or recruitment site.
- Users **must not** use corporate email for personal commercial activity, personal legal or medical correspondence, political campaigning, or mass personal distribution.
- Users **must not** subscribe their corporate address to personal mailing lists or newsletters unrelated to their professional role.
- Incidental personal messages (e.g. notifying a family member of a schedule change) are permitted, provided they are occasional, brief, and contain no attachment.
- Users **must not** auto-forward Company email to any personal or external mailbox. Forwarding rules to external addresses are blocked at the mail gateway and generate a security alert.
- Users **have 30 days** from the effective date of this policy to migrate any personal registrations away from their corporate address. After that date, continued personal registration will be treated as a policy violation under Section 8.

**4.2.3 Prohibited email conduct.** Users **must not**:

- Send Company confidential, restricted, or client data to any personal or unauthorized external address;
- Use a Company address to send harassing, discriminatory, threatening, defamatory, or obscene content;
- Impersonate another person, spoof a sender address, or send anonymous mail;
- Send chain mail, spam, or unsolicited bulk mail;
- Open attachments or follow links in unexpected or suspicious messages — these **must** be reported using the "Report Phishing" button;
- Disable, alter, or bypass email security controls, encryption, or the corporate disclaimer;
- Use personal email accounts to conduct Company business, including sending or receiving work documents.

**4.2.4 Classification and encryption.** Every outbound email **must** carry a sensitivity label. Messages classified **Confidential** or **Restricted** **must** be encrypted in transit and **must not** be sent to external recipients without the approval of the data owner. Client source code and credentials **must never** be sent by email in any form.

**4.2.5 Retention.** Corporate email is retained under the Data Retention Schedule (STD-LEG-001):

| Mailbox / Category | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Standard user mailbox — all items | 7 years from date of send/receipt |
| Deleted items | Recoverable for 30 days, then purged |
| Executive and Legal mailboxes | 10 years |
| Contract, financial, and client-commitment correspondence | 10 years from contract end |
| Mailboxes subject to a Legal Hold | Retained indefinitely until the hold is lifted by Legal |
| Departed User mailbox | Converted to shared/inactive mailbox for 90 days, then archived for the balance of the standard period |

Users **must not** delete, alter, or archive email that is, or that they reasonably believe may become, subject to a Legal Hold. Users **must not** use personal archives (PST files, local exports) to retain Company mail outside the managed system.

**4.2.6 Monitoring.** Email content and metadata are subject to automated scanning for malware, phishing, and data loss prevention (DLP) triggers. Human review of message content occurs only under a documented investigation authorized jointly by the CISO and HR, or where required by law.

### 4.3 Software and Application Use

**4.3.1 Approved software only.** Users **may** install and run only software listed in the **Approved Software Catalogue**, published on the intranet and maintained by IT Operations. The Catalogue records, for each entry, the approved version range, licence terms, approved use cases, and any data-handling restrictions.

**4.3.2 Administrative rights.** Standard Users do not hold local administrator rights on Company devices. Software installation is performed through the Company Software Centre (self-service, for pre-approved titles) or by IT Operations. Developers requiring elevated rights on development machines **must** hold an approved Developer Elevation entitlement, which is time-bound, logged, and reviewed quarterly; that entitlement **does not** authorize installation of software outside the Catalogue.

**4.3.3 Request and approval procedure.** To introduce new software, a User **must**:

1. Submit a Software Request through the Service Desk, stating the business need, the vendor, the version, the data it will process, and the intended user population.
2. Await **IT Operations** review of compatibility, licensing, and support (target: 5 business days).
3. Await **Information Security** review — vulnerability history, vendor security posture, telemetry and data residency, and where applicable a Data Protection Impact Assessment (target: 10 business days for standard requests).
4. Await **Legal/Procurement** review of licence terms and, where the software processes Company or client data, execution of a data processing agreement.
5. Receive written approval and installation by IT Operations. **Users must not install the software themselves in the interim.**

Expedited review (48 hours) is available for genuine business-critical needs, requested by a department head.

**4.3.4 Specifically prohibited.** Users **must not**, on any Company system or on a BYOD device with access to Company data:

- Install unlicensed, pirated, cracked, or key-generated software;
- Install software downloaded from unofficial sources, torrents, or file-sharing sites;
- Install personal-use software (games, media applications, personal utilities) on Company devices;
- Install remote access, remote control, or screen-sharing tools other than those in the Catalogue;
- Install browser extensions outside the approved extension allowlist — browser extensions have read access to every page the User visits and are a recognized data-exfiltration vector;
- Introduce hacking, penetration-testing, network-scanning, packet-capture, or password-cracking tools, except by members of the Security Team acting under written authorization;
- Connect the Company environment to an unapproved third-party SaaS service, including through OAuth "Sign in with…" grants or marketplace integrations;
- Use unapproved AI coding assistants or LLM services with Company or client source code, data, or credentials (see 4.6.6);
- Disable, uninstall, or interfere with endpoint protection, EDR, MDM, disk encryption, or logging agents.

**4.3.5 Open-source components.** Open-source libraries and dependencies incorporated into TechSecure products **must** be introduced through the approved package registries and dependency-scanning pipeline, and **must** carry a licence on the approved licence list (see STD-ENG-004 Open Source Governance Standard). Copyleft licences (GPL, AGPL) require Legal approval before use in any distributed or client-facing product.

**4.3.6 Discovery and remediation.** IT Operations maintains automated software inventory across all managed endpoints. Unapproved software identified by inventory **will** be removed, and the User notified. Repeated installation of unapproved software after notification is treated as a moderate violation under Section 8. Users who have already installed unapproved software **may** disclose it through the Service Desk within **30 days** of this policy's effective date without disciplinary consequence, under a one-time amnesty; the software will be assessed and either approved or removed.

### 4.4 Social Media

**4.4.1 Confidentiality — the standing issue.** The Company has identified disclosure of confidential project information on social media as a material and recurring risk. Regardless of platform, privacy settings, audience size, or intent, Users **must not** publish, post, share, comment on, or otherwise disclose:

- The existence, name, scope, timeline, status, or technical details of any unreleased product, feature, or project;
- The identity of any client, or any information from which a client's identity could be inferred, without prior written approval from Marketing **and** the account owner;
- Source code, architecture diagrams, configuration, infrastructure details, API keys, internal URLs, or screenshots of internal systems;
- Internal metrics, financial information, headcount, roadmaps, incidents, outages, or security vulnerabilities;
- Photographs or video taken inside Company premises where screens, whiteboards, or printed materials are visible;
- Information about a colleague, including their role, location, or contact details, without their consent;
- Job postings, team growth, or reorganizations that reveal unannounced strategic direction.

**4.4.2 The default rule.** If information has not been published on an official TechSecure channel, it is not public. A User who is unsure **must** treat it as confidential and consult Marketing or the Information Security Team before posting.

**4.4.3 Personal accounts and attribution.** Users are free to maintain personal social media accounts and to identify TechSecure as their employer. Where a User's profile identifies their employment, or where their post concerns the Company's industry, products, or clients, the User **must**:

- Speak only for themselves and include a clear disclaimer such as *"Views are my own and do not represent TechSecure Solutions Inc."*;
- Never state or imply that they speak on the Company's behalf;
- Not use the Company logo, brand assets, or trade marks as personal profile imagery or in personal content;
- Not respond to media enquiries, analyst questions, customer complaints, or public criticism of the Company — these **must** be referred to `press@techsecure.com` without reply;
- Not engage in arguments, harassment, or discriminatory conduct that could be attributed to the Company.

**4.4.4 Official Company accounts.** Only individuals designated in writing by the Head of Marketing may post to official TechSecure accounts. Credentials **must** be held in the enterprise password manager, protected by MFA, and revoked within **4 hours** of a designated poster leaving the role.

**4.4.5 Protected activity.** Nothing in this section restricts a User's legal right to discuss their own terms and conditions of employment, pay, or working conditions; to engage in lawful concerted or trade-union activity; or to make a protected disclosure to a regulator or under whistleblower legislation. Those rights are expressly preserved and are not affected by the confidentiality requirements above.

**4.4.6 Duration.** The confidentiality obligations in 4.4.1 survive the end of employment or engagement and remain binding indefinitely, in accordance with the User's confidentiality agreement.

**4.4.7 Reporting.** A User who becomes aware of confidential Company information published on any public platform — whether posted by themselves, a colleague, or a third party — **must** report it to `security@techsecure.com` within **1 hour** of discovery. Prompt self-reporting of one's own inadvertent disclosure is treated as a mitigating factor under Section 8.

### 4.5 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

**4.5.1 BYOD is a privilege requiring prior registration — the standing issue.** The Company has identified unregistered personal devices connecting to the corporate network as a material risk. Effective on this policy's date, **no personal device may connect to a corporate network or access Company data until it has been registered and enrolled** under this section. Network Access Control (NAC) will quarantine unregistered devices on the corporate SSID and wired ports; the guest network remains available for unregistered personal devices, and carries no access to Company systems.

**4.5.2 Eligibility.** BYOD participation requires: a business justification, written approval from the User's line manager, and approval from IT Security. Certain roles — those with access to client production data, regulated data, or privileged infrastructure — are **not eligible** for BYOD and **must** use Company-issued devices exclusively.

**4.5.3 Registration procedure.** Before first connection, the User **must**:

1. Submit a BYOD Registration Request through the Service Desk (device make, model, OS version, serial/IMEI, intended use).
2. Obtain manager and IT Security approval.
3. Enrol the device in the Company Mobile Device Management (MDM) / Unified Endpoint Management platform.
4. Pass an automated compliance check against the requirements in 4.5.4.
5. Read and sign the **BYOD Participation Agreement** (Annex B), which sets out the Company's remote-wipe rights and the limits of Company access to the device.

**4.5.4 Minimum security requirements.** A registered personal device **must** at all times:

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Operating system | Vendor-supported version, currently receiving security updates; security patches applied within **14 days** of release |
| Integrity | Not jailbroken, rooted, or running an unlocked bootloader or custom ROM |
| Screen lock | Biometric or PIN/passphrase of at least 6 digits / 8 characters; auto-lock after **5 minutes** of inactivity |
| Encryption | Full-device encryption enabled |
| Anti-malware | Company-approved endpoint protection installed and current (laptops/desktops) |
| Containerization | Company data confined to the MDM-managed work profile/container; copy, paste, screenshot, backup, and share operations restricted between the work container and personal applications |
| Authentication | MFA enrolled for all Company services accessed from the device |
| Network | Company VPN active for all access to internal systems |
| Backup | Company data **must not** be backed up to personal cloud accounts (iCloud, Google backup) |
| Sharing | Device **must not** be shared with, or left unattended in the presence of, family members or any other person while Company data is accessible |

**4.5.5 Company rights over registered devices.** By registering a device the User authorizes the Company to: enforce the configuration in 4.5.4; inventory installed applications relevant to security posture; **selectively wipe** the Company work container upon device loss, upon detection of non-compliance, or upon the User's departure; and, where a selective wipe is not technically possible on that device class, perform a **full device wipe** as a last resort in a confirmed data-loss scenario. The Company **does not** access personal photographs, personal messages, personal email, personal browsing history, or location data on registered personal devices.

**4.5.6 Loss, theft, and change of device.** Users **must** report the loss or theft of any device holding Company data to the Service Desk **within 1 hour** of discovery, at any hour of the day. Users **must** de-register a device and confirm removal of Company data before selling, transferring, trading in, or disposing of it, and **must** register any replacement device before use.

**4.5.7 Departure.** On termination of employment or engagement, the Company work container **will** be wiped and device registration revoked. The User **must** make the device available for verification of removal if requested. Retention of Company data on a personal device after departure is a severe violation under Section 8 and may constitute misappropriation of Company property.

**4.5.8 Cost and support.** The Company does not reimburse the purchase, repair, replacement, or data plan of personal devices, and provides support only for Company applications and the work container — not for the device or its personal software.

**4.5.9 Peripherals and removable media.** Personal USB storage devices, external drives, and memory cards **must not** be connected to Company systems. USB mass storage is blocked by endpoint policy. Where removable media is required for a genuine business need, IT Operations issues encrypted, Company-managed media under an approved request.

### 4.6 Data Handling

**4.6.1 Classification.** All Company and client information is classified under the Data Classification Standard (STD-SEC-006). Users **must** apply the correct label at creation and handle information accordingly:

| Classification | Definition | Storage | Sharing | Retention / Disposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Approved for external release | Any approved Company system | Unrestricted after Marketing approval | As per business need |
| **Internal** | General business information; harmful only in aggregate | Approved Company cloud storage and collaboration platforms | Internal only; external sharing needs manager approval | 3 years default; standard deletion |
| **Confidential** | Source code, product roadmaps, financials, personal data of staff | Approved encrypted Company repositories and storage; encryption in transit and at rest | Named individuals with business need; external sharing requires data owner approval and an NDA | Per Retention Schedule; secure deletion; certificate of destruction for physical media |
| **Restricted** | Client production data, credentials, security keys, regulated personal data, unreleased M&A information | Designated secure enclaves and secrets managers only; never on endpoints; never on BYOD | Explicit written authorization of the data owner and CISO; access logged and time-limited | Per contractual and regulatory requirement; cryptographic erasure; destruction certified and recorded |

**4.6.2 Storage.** Company data **must** be stored only in approved Company-managed systems. Users **must not** store Company data on personal cloud accounts, personal email, personal devices outside a managed container, unencrypted removable media, unmanaged local folders, or unapproved third-party services. Local copies **must** be minimized; where a local working copy is unavoidable, full-disk encryption **must** be enabled and the copy deleted when no longer required.

**4.6.3 Sharing and transfer.** Users **must**:

- Share by granting access within the approved platform (named recipients, least privilege, expiry date) rather than by attaching or exporting files;
- Verify recipient identity and business need before granting access, and re-verify external recipients out of band;
- Never create "anyone with the link" shares for Internal, Confidential, or Restricted data;
- Never transfer Company data to a personal account "to work on at home" — remote access is provided for that purpose;
- Obtain data owner approval and a signed NDA before any external disclosure of Confidential data;
- Route all client data transfers through the approved secure file transfer service.

**4.6.4 Access.** Users **must** access only the data necessary for their assigned duties. Curiosity, convenience, and "I had the permission" are not business justifications. Access rights are reviewed quarterly; Users **must** report to their manager any access they hold but no longer need.

**4.6.5 Disposal.** Data and media **must** be disposed of securely:

| Medium | Method |
|---|---|
| Electronic data in Company systems | Deletion through the platform, subject to the Retention Schedule and any Legal Hold |
| Endpoint drives and Company devices | Cryptographic erasure by IT Operations; devices sanitized to NIST SP 800-88 standards before reuse, return, or disposal |
| Cloud storage and backups | Deletion via the managed service, with confirmation that backups and snapshots are purged on their defined cycle |
| Removable media | Cryptographic erasure; physical destruction where the media held Restricted data |
| Paper documents | Cross-cut shredding using the secure bins at each office; Confidential and Restricted paper **must never** be placed in general waste or recycling |
| Whiteboards | Erased at the end of every meeting where project information was written |

Users working remotely **must** return Confidential and Restricted paper documents to an office for secure destruction, or use a cross-cut shredder — household waste is not an acceptable disposal route.

**4.6.6 Artificial intelligence and third-party services.** Users **must not** submit Company or client source code, data, credentials, or confidential information to any AI service, code assistant, translation service, online formatter, converter, debugger, or similar third-party tool that is not on the Approved Software Catalogue with an explicit approval for that data classification. Approved AI tooling and its permitted data classifications are listed in the Catalogue and governed by STD-SEC-012.

**4.6.7 Physical security of information.** Users **must** maintain a clear desk and clear screen: lock the screen when leaving a workstation, position screens away from public view when working in public spaces, collect printed output immediately, and secure Confidential and Restricted material in a locked drawer overnight. Company visitors **must** be escorted at all times and **must not** be left in areas where screens or documents are visible.

**4.6.8 Incident reporting.** Users **must** report any actual or suspected loss, unauthorized disclosure, unauthorized access, corruption, or misdirection of Company or client data **within 1 hour of discovery**, using the channels in Section 6. This includes email sent to the wrong recipient, a file shared with the wrong person, and data pasted into the wrong window. Early reporting is what makes containment possible; Section 8.5 protects Users who report their own errors in good faith.

## 5. Monitoring and Privacy

5.1 TechSecure monitors and logs use of its information systems for the following purposes only: protecting the security of systems and data; detecting and investigating security incidents; preventing data loss; verifying compliance with this policy; meeting legal, regulatory, and client contractual obligations; and maintaining system performance and capacity.

5.2 Monitoring in operation includes: network and DNS traffic logs; endpoint detection and response telemetry; authentication and access logs; email and file DLP scanning; cloud and repository audit logs; software inventory; and, on BYOD, compliance state of the managed container only.

5.3 Automated monitoring is continuous. **Review of an individual User's activity by a person** occurs only where a documented investigation is authorized jointly by the CISO and the Head of HR, where a serious and specific concern exists, or where the Company is required to do so by law or by legal process. Such review is proportionate to the concern and limited to the data necessary to resolve it.

5.4 Logs are retained for 12 months by default, and longer where required by a Legal Hold, contractual obligation, or ongoing investigation.

5.5 Users are entitled to be informed about the personal data the Company holds about them and to exercise their rights under applicable data protection law; the Employee Privacy Notice (POL-HR-009) explains how.

5.6 By using Company information systems, Users acknowledge this monitoring. A login banner restating this notice is displayed on all Company systems.

## 6. Incident Reporting

6.1 Users **must** report the following **within 1 hour of discovery**, without first attempting to investigate or remediate:

- Suspected or confirmed data loss, disclosure, or misdirection
- Lost or stolen devices containing Company data
- Suspected malware, ransomware, or unusual system behaviour
- Phishing, vishing, smishing, or social engineering attempts
- Suspected account compromise or an MFA prompt the User did not initiate
- Confidential Company information observed on a public platform
- Any observed violation of this policy, by any person

6.2 Reporting channels:

| Channel | Detail | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Service Desk portal | `https://servicedesk.techsecure.com` | 24/7 |
| Security email | `security@techsecure.com` | 24/7, monitored |
| Security hotline | ext. **4357** / +XXX XXX XXXX | 24/7 |
| Phishing report | "Report Phishing" button in the mail client | Immediate |
| Anonymous / whistleblower line | `https://techsecure.ethicspoint.com` | 24/7, independently operated |

6.3 Users **must not** delay a report in order to determine whether the incident is "real". Assessment is the Security Team's responsibility; false alarms carry no penalty.

| **Executive Management** | Approve this policy; allocate resources for its implementation and enforcement; comply with it visibly and without exception. |

## 8. Enforcement and Consequences

8.1 **Binding effect.** Compliance with this policy is a condition of access to TechSecure information systems and, for employees, a term of employment. For contractors and third parties, compliance is a contractual obligation.

8.2 **Immediate protective measures.** Independently of any disciplinary process, the Company may at any time and without prior notice: suspend or restrict a User's access; quarantine or disconnect a device; remove unapproved software; wipe a BYOD work container; and preserve and examine relevant logs and data. These are protective, not punitive, measures and do not imply a finding of fault.

8.3 **Investigation.** Suspected violations are investigated by the Information Security Team jointly with HR. The User will be informed of the allegation and given a fair opportunity to respond, except where doing so would prejudice an investigation into suspected criminal conduct or would risk the destruction of evidence. Findings are documented and retained.

8.4 **Consequences.** Sanctions are proportionate to severity, intent, harm caused, and prior record:

| Tier | Illustrative Conduct | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — Minor** | Occasional personal browsing beyond the permitted limit; a single unlabelled email; late completion of awareness training; failing to lock a screen | Documented verbal coaching by the line manager; remedial training; correction required within 5 business days |
| **Tier 2 — Moderate** | Continued personal use of corporate email after the migration deadline; installing unapproved software; connecting an unregistered personal device; sharing an Internal file externally without approval; repeated Tier 1 conduct | Formal written warning recorded on the personnel file; access restrictions pending remediation; mandatory retraining; ineligibility for BYOD for 12 months |
| **Tier 3 — Serious** | Posting confidential project information on social media; disabling security controls; sending Confidential data to a personal account; sharing credentials; failing to report a known incident; repeated Tier 2 conduct | Final written warning or suspension; removal of elevated privileges; possible demotion or reassignment; disciplinary process up to and including dismissal |
| **Tier 4 — Severe / Malicious** | Deliberate theft or exfiltration of source code or client data; unauthorized access to systems or colleagues' accounts; installation of malware or unauthorized surveillance tools; accessing illegal material; retaining Company data after departure; falsifying records or obstructing an investigation | Immediate suspension and revocation of all access; summary dismissal or immediate contract termination; referral to law enforcement; civil action for recovery of loss; notification to clients and regulators where required |

8.5 **Good-faith reporting and self-disclosure.** A User who promptly and honestly reports their own error, or reports a concern about another person in good faith, **will not** be disciplined for making the report, and their self-disclosure **will** be treated as a significant mitigating factor in any consequence assessment. Concealment, delay, or falsification, by contrast, is an aggravating factor and is itself a Tier 3 violation. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is a Tier 3 violation.

8.6 **Third parties.** Violations by contractors, vendors, or partners are escalated to the engaging manager and Vendor Management, and may result in removal of individual personnel from the engagement, suspension of the contract, or termination for breach.

8.7 **Legal consequences.** Nothing in this policy limits the Company's right to pursue civil remedies or to refer conduct to law enforcement. Certain conduct covered by this policy may independently constitute a criminal offence.

8.8 **Fair process.** Disciplinary action is administered by HR in accordance with POL-HR-002 (Disciplinary Procedure) and applicable employment law, including the User's right to representation and to appeal.

## 9. Compliance Measurement

9.1 The Information Security Team measures compliance through: automated software and device inventory; NAC and MDM compliance reporting; DLP and web-filter event review; email rule and forwarding audits; quarterly access reviews; training completion reporting; and periodic simulated phishing exercises.

9.2 Reporting targets:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Policy acknowledgement (all Users) | 100% within 14 days of hire and annually |
| Security awareness training completion | 100% annually |
| Registered/compliant devices on corporate networks | 100% |
| Unapproved software instances outstanding > 30 days | 0 |
| Incidents reported within the 1-hour requirement | ≥ 95% |
| Simulated phishing report rate | ≥ 70% reported; ≤ 5% credential submission |

9.3 Metrics are reported to the CISO monthly and to the Executive Management Committee quarterly.

## 10. Exceptions

10.1 Any deviation from this policy requires a documented exception request to the Information Security Team stating the requirement to be excepted, the business justification, the systems, data, and Users affected, the residual risk, the compensating controls, and a proposed expiry date.

10.2 Exceptions require approval by the CISO; exceptions with High residual risk additionally require CEO approval. No exception exceeds **12 months** without re-justification.

10.3 The Information Security Team maintains a central exception register, reviewed quarterly by the CISO and annually by Internal Audit.

10.4 No exception may be granted to the prohibitions on illegal content (4.1.3), circumvention of security controls (3.5), or retention of Company data after departure (4.5.7).

## 11. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Acceptable Use** | Use of Company information systems consistent with this policy and with the business purpose for which access was granted. |
| **BYOD** | Bring Your Own Device — the use of a personally owned device to access Company data or networks. |
| **Company Data** | Any information created, received, processed, or stored by or on behalf of TechSecure, including client data entrusted to it. |
| **Confidential Information** | Information classified Confidential or Restricted under the Data Classification Standard, including source code, roadmaps, financials, client data, and personal data. |
| **Containerization / Work Profile** | Technical separation of Company applications and data from personal applications and data on a single device, enforced by MDM. |
| **DLP (Data Loss Prevention)** | Technology that detects and blocks the transmission of sensitive data outside authorized channels. |
| **EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)** | Software that monitors endpoints for malicious activity and enables investigation and containment. |
| **Legal Hold** | A directive from Legal requiring the preservation of specified records for litigation or investigation; it overrides all deletion and retention schedules. |
| **MDM / UEM** | Mobile Device Management / Unified Endpoint Management — the platform used to enforce security configuration on managed devices. |
| **MFA** | Multi-Factor Authentication — authentication requiring two or more independent factors. |
| **NAC** | Network Access Control — technology that verifies device identity and compliance before permitting network access. |
| **Removable Media** | USB drives, external disks, memory cards, optical media, and any portable storage device. |
| **Selective Wipe** | Removal of Company data and applications from a device, leaving personal data intact. |
| **Sanitization** | Rendering data on media irrecoverable, to the standards of NIST SP 800-88. |
| **User** | Any individual within the scope of Section 2.1. |
| **Must / Shall** | A mandatory requirement; non-compliance is a policy violation. |
| **Should** | A recommendation; deviation should be justifiable but is not itself a violation. |
| **May** | A permitted option. |
